Support

Duodoodle is a two-player game on one iPhone or iPad. Sit on opposite sides of the device. One of you draws a secret word; the other guesses out loud or by typing. Then you switch.

A few things worth knowing

  • The game is built for portrait, with players on opposite edges of the device.
  • Voice guessing needs the microphone. If you turn that down, use Type a guess on the guesser’s side.
  • Nothing in the app needs an internet connection. If a prompt appears, you can dismiss it and keep playing.
  • Player names, timers, categories, and difficulty live in Settings, from the home screen.
