Privacy

Duodoodle does not collect personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising identifiers. The app does not send your drawings, guesses, or player names anywhere.

Voice guessing

If you allow it, Duodoodle uses the microphone so the guesser can say the word out loud. Speech is recognized on this device. It is not stored, and it is not sent to a server.

You can decline the microphone prompt and still play. The guesser’s side has a typing fallback.

What stays on the device

  • Player names, settings, and scores you set in the app
  • Drawings from the current game, until the round or game ends
  • Microphone audio, only while a guess is being heard, and only on the device

This website

This site is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not set cookies or collect information about you.
